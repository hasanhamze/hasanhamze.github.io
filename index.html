What happens while you sleep

A typical finance report automation, from the accounting system to a finished workbook. Nothing in the chain needs a person.

  1. 2:00 AM

    QuickBooks sends the report

    Your saved report goes out on a schedule. No one clicks export.

  2. 2:01 AM

    Power Automate files it

    A flow watches the inbox, picks up the attachment, and saves it to your SharePoint.

  3. 2:02 AM

    Power Query refreshes Excel

    The workbook pulls in the new file, cleans it, maps it, and fills your layout.

  4. 8:30 AM

    You open the workbook

    The numbers updated hours ago. Your morning starts with the analysis.

Questions teams ask first

HH Insights is a Toronto data and automation practice. We work with finance and operations teams that have outgrown manual spreadsheets but do not want another software platform.

Do we need to buy new software?

No. The automation runs on QuickBooks and the Microsoft 365 tools you already have: Excel, Power Query, Power Automate, and SharePoint.

Does our financial data leave the company?

No. Files move between your accounting system, your mailbox, and your SharePoint. Nothing is stored on our systems or sent to a third party.

What happens if an export format changes?

Monthly care covers it. We monitor each refresh, and if a changed column or layout breaks the pipeline, we fix it.

We do not use QuickBooks. Can you still help?

Usually, yes. If your system can export or email a report on a schedule, the same approach works. Tell us what you use on the review call.

What do you need from us to start?

A copy of the report as you build it today, a sample export from your accounting system, and someone who can answer questions about the logic behind it.

Can we change the report after it is automated?

Yes. Small additions such as new columns are part of monthly care. Larger changes are quoted separately, and because you own the files, your own team can edit them too.
